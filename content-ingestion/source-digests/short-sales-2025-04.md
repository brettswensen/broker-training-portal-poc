## Key Broker Guidance

### What is a Short Sale?
- Property value is less than debt owed
- Requires creditor/third party approval to accept less than full amount
- Third parties: mortgage lenders, insurers, bankruptcy trustees, taxing authorities

### Why Sellers Choose Short Sales
- Easier on credit than foreclosure
- Faster credit recovery
- Lower deficiency amount
- Seller typically stays in home and maintains it

### Alternatives to Explore First
- Negotiate commissions
- Buyer pays closing costs (or lender via higher rate)
- Loan modification
- Deed in lieu of foreclosure
- Bankruptcy consultation (refer to attorney)

### The Approval Process
1. **Loan Servicing Department** - Handles foreclosures, NOT your advocate
   - Incentivized on calls per hour, not customer satisfaction
   - Will lose paperwork repeatedly
   - Requires authorization forms (often wet signature)
   
2. **Loss Mitigation Department** - Minimizes lender losses
   - Formula-driven, stack of files, don't care about you
   - **KEY TOOL: Conversation log** - name, time, discussion, promises, requests
   - Can accelerate process from 6 months to 60 days

### Timeline Reality
- Shortest: 2 days
- Longest: 1+ years
- Current market: 2-4 months
- **Guarantee: Something unreasonable WILL happen**

### Buyer Perspective
- Typically 4-6% discount off market price
- Fewer buyers willing to deal with uncertainty
- Seller can cancel anytime before bank approval
- Continue looking at other properties while waiting

### Commission Impact
- Banks often dictate maximum commission
- Common: 6% cut to 5% total
- No negotiation on bank's commission decision

### Critical Forms (Utah)
1. Short Sale Disclosure
2. Addendum to Listing Agreement
3. **Short Sale Addendum to REPC** - NEVER accept without this
4. Backup Addendum
5. Acknowledgment of Third Party Approval

### Key Contract Clauses
- Short sale approval deadline (contract lapses if not approved)
- Earnest money timing (seller wants immediate, buyer wants to wait)
- Shorter-than-normal contract deadlines
- Multiple backup offers allowed

## Objections and Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Why not just walk away? | Short sale easier on credit, lower deficiency, faster recovery |
| Bank won't approve | Banks prefer short sales to foreclosure; persistence wins |
| Taking too long | You CAN cancel and look elsewhere; that's the protection |
| Seller won't do repairs | Normal in short sales; plan to handle post-closing |

## Key Broker Guidance

### The Core Framework
- **Client = Hero** (Luke Skywalker, Frodo, Harry Potter)
- **Agent = Mystical Guide** (Yoda, Gandalf, Dumbledore, Mr. Miyagi)
- If you confuse, you lose — keep it simple

### The 7-Step Brand Script

1. **Aspirational Identity** — Who they are before vs after your help
   - Example: Before = overwhelmed homeowner with crappy yard; After = pride of neighborhood who doesn't lift a finger

2. **Character Wants Something** — Identify their desire

3. **Problem/Roadblock** — Keep talking about THEIR problem
   - When you stop talking about their problem, they stop listening
   - Ask deep questions: frustrated, cramped, injustice, "I deserve better"

4. **Meet the Guide** — Show empathy + express confidence
   - Show you understand and care
   - Show without shadow of a doubt you know how to help

5. **Simple Plan** — 3-4 steps max
   - Good consultation takes 1.5-2 hours
   - Most time spent understanding roadblock and desire, not explaining

6. **Call to Action** — Tell them exactly what to do
   - Emails with CTAs get 1600% better sales (per Marty, unverified)
   - Examples: "Call me," "Request a CMA," "Book an appointment"
   - Websites should have "Book an appointment" buttons everywhere, not just brochure content

7. **Paint Success Picture** — Use "Just imagine..."
   - "Just imagine having family over in that great big kitchen..."
   - If they can see it in their minds, they're more likely to buy

8. **Avoid Failure** — Briefly show what happens if they don't act
   - "I would hate for you to still be making that commute a year from now"
   - Spend WAY more time on success than failure

### Story Delivery Formats
- **Email:** Darren's strength — awesome email campaigns
- **Social media:** Heather's strength — "I want to tell you a story" gets most comments
- **One-on-one:** Paula's strength — exceptional at group and individual storytelling
- **General hook:** "Story time" — two words that got huge attention for one Instagrammer

### Practical Applications

**Buyer/Seller Consultation:**
- Start with: "Tell me what made you think about moving. What's not working?"
- Spend 1.5-2 hours, mostly listening
- Present 3-4 step plan
- End with clear next step (call to action)

**Email Campaigns:**
- Start with "I want to tell you a story"
- Tell client transformation stories
- Include clear CTA every time

**Social Media:**
- Story posts get most engagement
- Use "Story time" as hook
- Focus on problem → solution narrative

## Key Broker Guidance

### The #1 Fatal Mistake

**Trying to 1031 after already closing:**
> "I've already sold it. I've already got the check in hand. I can't 1031 exchange that."

If the client has closed and received proceeds without a qualified intermediary, the opportunity is permanently lost. The QI must be engaged BEFORE closing.

### What Is a 1031 Exchange?

- Section 1031 of the IRS code governs capital gains taxes
- It's a **deferral** process, not elimination
- Taxes get "kicked down the road"
- IRS created this to keep investors in the market (not getting hit with huge tax bills that prevent reinvestment)

### Capital Gains Tax Rates

| Holding Period | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Short-term (< 1 year) | 10-37% | Ordinary income rates |
| Long-term (> 1 year) | 0-20% | Depending on income bracket |

Flippers pay up to 37% - this is "baked into their math."

### The Three Critical Rules

**1. Qualified Intermediary Required**
- Must be engaged BEFORE closing
- Cannot be the agent, attorney, or accountant
- Holds proceeds between sales
- Title company must be aware

**2. 45-Day Identification Rule**
- From closing of relinquished property
- Must identify replacement properties IN WRITING
- Delivered to qualified intermediary
- Three options:
  - **Rule A:** Up to 3 properties of ANY value
  - **Rule B:** Any number, but total value ≤ 200% of sold property
  - **Rule C:** Any number if acquiring 95% of identified value

**3. 180-Day Closing Rule**
- Must CLOSE on replacement within 180 calendar days
- Hard deadline - no extensions
- Must close, not just be under contract

### Like-Kind Property

Very broad for real estate:
- Rental house → Commercial building ✓
- Land → Apartment complex ✓
- Duplex → Office building ✓

**Does NOT include:**
- Primary residence
- Property held primarily for sale (flips)
- Must be investment or business property

### Boot Rules

"Boot" = cash or debt relief taken out of the exchange

**To fully defer ALL taxes:**
1. Buy replacement property equal or GREATER in value
2. Reinvest ALL net equity
3. Replace ALL debt

**Partial exchange example:**
- Sell for $500K, buy for $450K, take $50K cash
- Pay tax on $50K (boot)
- Defer tax on remaining $450K

### Utah Disclosure

Utah has a specific form for disclosing when a property being sold is part of a 1031 exchange. The buyer must be informed.

## Client-Facing Language

**Explaining the concept:**
> "A 1031 exchange lets you defer paying capital gains tax by reinvesting the proceeds into another investment property. You're not eliminating the tax - just kicking it down the road."

**When client already sold:**
> "If you've already closed and have the check in hand, we cannot 1031 exchange that. You needed to set this up before closing with a qualified intermediary."

**Setting expectations:**
> "Before we go any further, we need to get a qualified intermediary involved and make sure the title company knows this is a 1031 exchange. Your accountant should also be looped in."

**On timelines:**
> "You have 45 days from closing to identify replacement properties in writing. Then 180 days to close on the new one. No extensions, no exceptions."

**On taking cash out:**
> "If you take cash out of the deal, that portion is taxable. To defer ALL taxes, you need to buy a property equal or greater in value and put all your equity into it."

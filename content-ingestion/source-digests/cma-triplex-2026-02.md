## Key Broker Guidance

### Multi-Unit Property Financing

**The Cross-Collateralization Problem:**
Standard residential lenders (Fannie/Freddie) will NOT do one loan across multiple properties.

**Options:**
1. **Separate loans:** Two loans, two refis, two sets of disclosures
2. **Portfolio lender:** Holds loans in-house, may "break traditional rules"
3. **DSCR lender:** Investment-focused, may allow it - must verify

**What to Tell Investor Clients:**
> "A standard lender won't do one loan across multiple properties. We'll need separate financing for each unit, or find a portfolio lender who holds loans in-house."

### Unpermitted Additions

**Valuation Rule:**
- Permitted SF: Full market rate
- Unpermitted SF: 50% or less of market rate

**Disclosure Requirements:**
- Must disclose unpermitted status to buyer
- Buyer should verify with city what permitting would require
- Some buyers won't touch unpermitted properties
- You'll have to disclose when you sell

**Client Language:**
> "The unpermitted square footage is valued at a discount - typically 50% or less of the permitted space value. We need to verify with the city what it would take to permit it."

### Short-Term Rental Income Verification

**Never Trust Seller's Word:**
- Get actual Airbnb dashboard screenshots
- Or tax returns showing rental income
- Look for seasonal variations
- Calculate NET, not gross

**Costs to Factor:**
- Management fees (20-30% typical)
- Cleaning between guests
- Utilities
- Platform fees (Airbnb, VRBO)
- Maintenance/wear and tear

**Backup Analysis:**
- Compare to long-term rental rates
- Calculate break-even occupancy rate
- Stress-test with lower occupancy

### Comping Unique Properties

**When Standard Comps Don't Exist:**
1. Expand search radius (3-5 miles vs 1 mile)
2. Extend time frame (12-18 months vs 6 months)
3. Look for similar USE CASES, not just structures
4. Consider valuing as: Primary home + ADU income
5. Document your rationale
6. Be transparent about limitations

### ADU Regulations

**City-Specific Rules Vary:**
- Short-term rentals allowed in ADU? (Some yes, some no)
- Parking requirements
- Owner-occupancy requirements
- Size limitations
- Utility separations

**Always verify CURRENT ordinance before:**
- Marketing rental income potential
- Calculating ROI for investor
- Writing offers with ADU income factored

## Client-Facing Language

**On multi-unit financing:**
> "A standard lender won't do one loan across multiple properties. We'll need separate financing for each unit, or find a portfolio lender who holds loans in-house."

**On unpermitted space:**
> "The unpermitted square footage is valued at a discount - typically 50% or less of the permitted space value. We need to verify with the city what it would take to permit it, and you'll need to disclose this when you sell."

**On Airbnb income:**
> "We need actual Airbnb income documentation, not just what the seller tells us. I'll want to see dashboard screenshots or tax returns to verify the numbers."

**On unique comps:**
> "This is a unique property, so we're expanding our search to find the most comparable sales. We may need to value it as a primary home plus separate ADU income."

**On ADU regulations:**
> "ADU regulations vary by city. We need to verify that this property can legally operate as an ADU and whether short-term rentals are permitted before we factor that income into the valuation."

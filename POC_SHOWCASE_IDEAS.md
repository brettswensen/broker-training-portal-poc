# Broker Knowledge Base POC Showcase Ideas

## Core Demo Goal
Show a broker that this is not just a file library. It is a recruiting, onboarding, training, and daily-agent-support system powered by the brokerage's own knowledge.

The proof of concept should make a broker think:

> “If I bring agents onto my team, this gives them instant access to how we do things here — without me answering the same questions 100 times.”

---

## Hero Demo Flow

### 1. Search Bar First
Top of the dashboard should feature a large search bar:

```text
Search trainings, scripts, playbooks, objections, contracts...
```

Example searches:
- `listing appointment`
- `seller wants too much`
- `open house follow up`
- `buyer consultation script`
- `inspection objection`
- `new agent first week`

Search result cards should show:
- title
- content type: training, script, playbook, checklist, document
- short summary
- matched topic tags
- source/citation
- action buttons: View, Star, Add to Onboarding

---

## High-Value POC Modules

### 2. New Agent Onboarding Command Center
A broker-facing and agent-facing onboarding area.

Example sections:
- Day 1: brokerage overview, tools, expectations
- Week 1: CRM setup, scripts, first contacts
- First 30 Days: lead follow-up, buyer consult, listing prep
- First Deal: contracts, deadlines, inspection, appraisal, closing

Demo value:
- Broker can show recruits: “You will not be left alone. Everything is structured.”
- Agent can see progress and know exactly what to do next.

Cards:
- New Agent 30-Day Roadmap
- First 10 Conversations to Practice
- CRM Setup Checklist
- Buyer Consult Training
- Listing Appointment Training

---

### 3. Ask the Broker AI Assistant
An AI box that searches the indexed trainings and answers from the brokerage's own materials.

Label options:
- Ask the Broker
- Ask the Team Brain
- Ask the Training Library
- Ask [Brokerage Name]

Suggested prompt chips:
- How do I handle an overpriced seller?
- What should I do on my first week?
- Give me the open house follow-up process.
- What do I say to a buyer before showings?
- Where is the listing checklist?

Answer format:
1. Direct answer
2. Step-by-step process
3. Suggested script
4. Related trainings
5. Source citations

---

### 4. Scenario Coach
Turn the knowledge base into roleplay/training simulations.

Examples:
- Seller says: “Zillow says my home is worth more.”
- Buyer says: “I want to wait until rates drop.”
- Lead says: “I already have an agent.”
- Seller says: “Your commission is too high.”

The system responds with:
- what to say
- why it works
- related script
- related training
- practice mode / roleplay mode

Demo value:
This shows the portal as an agent development tool, not just a search tool.

---

### 5. Broker Playbooks
Curated guides synthesized from trainings, transcripts, SOPs, and scripts.

Recommended first playbooks:
- New Agent 30-Day Onboarding
- Listing Appointment Playbook
- Buyer Consultation Playbook
- Open House Lead Capture & Follow-Up
- Price Reduction Conversation Playbook
- Internet Lead Follow-Up Playbook
- Inspection Objection Playbook
- Contract-to-Close Checklist

Each playbook should include:
- overview
- steps
- scripts
- common mistakes
- related files/trainings
- source citations

---

### 6. “What Should I Do Today?” Agent Dashboard
A daily action layer that turns training into activity.

Example cards:
- Practice one script
- Watch one training
- Contact 10 sphere leads
- Follow up with open house visitors
- Review one contract concept

Demo value:
Great for recruiting because the broker can show agents a clear operating system.

---

### 7. Objection Library
A searchable objection-handling system.

Categories:
- commission objections
- pricing objections
- rate/payment objections
- timing objections
- loyalty/already-have-agent objections
- inspection/appraisal stress

Each objection page:
- exact objection
- recommended response
- shorter text-message response
- coaching notes
- related training source

---

### 8. Deal Desk / Transaction Help
A practical support area for active transactions.

Agent asks:
- What are the next steps after inspection?
- What do I do if appraisal comes in low?
- What does the seller disclosure deadline mean?
- How do I explain earnest money?

The system answers from internal process docs and training materials.

---

### 9. Recruit-Facing Demo Mode
A polished view a broker can show prospective agents.

Headline:
```text
Everything you need to ramp faster, sell smarter, and get answers instantly.
```

Proof points:
- structured onboarding
- searchable training library
- AI broker assistant
- scripts and playbooks
- progress tracking
- source-backed answers

---

## Best POC Screens to Build First

### Screen 1 — Dashboard/Home
- Big search bar
- Ask the Broker prompt box
- onboarding progress card
- popular playbooks
- recently added trainings
- topic cards

### Screen 2 — Search Results
- query: `seller wants too much`
- results include pricing strategy training, price reduction script, listing appointment playbook

### Screen 3 — Ask the Broker
- query: `How do I handle a seller who wants to overprice?`
- answer includes script + steps + source citations

### Screen 4 — New Agent Onboarding
- 30-day checklist
- required trainings
- scripts to practice
- progress states

### Screen 5 — Playbooks
- card grid like Operators
- each card shows sources, estimated time, topic/category

---

## Recommended POC Positioning

For brokers, the strongest pitch is:

> This turns your brokerage's training, scripts, documents, and experience into an always-on onboarding and coaching system for every agent you recruit.

Not just:

> Search your files.

Better:

> Clone your brokerage brain and make it usable by every agent on day one.
